# Changelog
## 1.11.2.1
- **Foundry v11 (D35E 2.4.3):** the same Adventurers and Minions runs on Foundry v11 as Axecleft's Adventurers and Minions for v11. Items added from a compendium remember it where Foundry v11 keeps that (so Edit recognises them), the A&M backups folder is made on v11 too, Export on Build profiles and Prestige requirements saves the file there, the window's pages fill and scroll in a v11 window, and the window's Tour button starts the tour of the page shown.

## 1.14.2.0
- **Adventurers and Minions, rebuilt.** The module once called "Adventurers and Minions v2" now replaces the first Adventurers and Minions: the same id (`axeclefts-adventurers-minions`), title and repository, so Update All installs it over 1.14.1.0. Build a character step by step, Random (one or many at once) and Edit an actor take over from the Ability Assigner and Class Assigner. Everything below (1.14.0.x) is part of it.
- **Your v2 test data carries over:** actors made or edited under the v2 id (`axeclefts-aam2`) still reopen exactly as built, and a world's v2 settings are copied to the new id the first time a GM loads it.
- **Recommends** Axecleft's Gemstones (Treasure Generator), Armory and Curios.
- **Rogue special abilities.** At rogue levels 10, 13, 16 and 19 the Feats step has a Rogue special ability slot: Crippling Strike, Defensive Roll, Improved Evasion, Opportunist, Skill Mastery or Slippery Mind (D35E's own class abilities, added to the actor), or a feat instead. Each ability can be taken once, except Skill Mastery, which asks for its skills (3 + the Intelligence modifier) and is named after them on the actor. The slot's list shows the abilities under **Special abilities**, then the feats under **Or a feat instead**. Random takes Crippling Strike, Improved Evasion, Opportunist and Defensive Roll in turn, and Edit reads the abilities A&M added back into their slots.
- **Random prestige classes:** Random's new **Prestige class** (None, Random once the character qualifies, or one class) gives the character a prestige class from the first level it meets the requirements (from 6th for a random one; the SRD's or your own), with the levels after it in that class up to its maximum. The skills and feats from then on are chosen again; the levels before stay. When none qualifies, the character keeps its classes and a note says so.
- **Owner** (GM): Review's Create and Random's Several at once can give the new actor to a player (they own it; the GM always does).
- **Recent:** the start page lists the last characters you made or edited (in this browser) with Edit and Sheet buttons.
- **Token controls:** an Adventurers and Minions button in the token tools opens A&M, or Edit on the selected token's actor when you own it.
- **Ranger combat style:** Classes and levels chooses a ranger's combat style, Archery or Two-Weapon Combat (its build's when you don't: the archer's or the two-weapon ranger's). On the actor A&M puts the style's features in place of D35E's description-only ones, named after it ("Combat Style (Archery)", "Improved Combat Style (Archery)", "Combat Style Mastery (Archery)"), each linking its feat, so D35E adds Rapid Shot, Manyshot and Improved Precise Shot (or Two-Weapon Fighting, Improved and Greater Two-Weapon Fighting) with it at 2nd, 6th and 11th level. The feats count for other feats' prerequisites and prestige classes, aren't offered again, and are listed on the Feats step. Medium or heavy armor in the ranger's gear is a warning (the style works only in light or no armor). Edit adds the next one at 6th and 11th, and changing the style swaps them, feats included.
- **Class feature variants** (Unearthed Arcana), chosen for each class on Classes and levels: whirling frenzy (barbarian rage; no greater or mighty rage or indomitable will, evasion while in a frenzy at 14th), favored environment (ranger, instead of favored enemy), turning undead by level check or planar banishment (cleric; level check for paladins too), and aspect of nature (druid, instead of wild shape). Totem barbarians and the other variant classes can take them too. What they give shows with the class features and goes on the actor as notes on the class; the features they replace leave the actor (D35E's own disabled abilities). Edit reads them back and changes them.
- **Specialist wizard variants** (Unearthed Arcana): once a wizard's specialist school is chosen (Spells), Classes and levels offers its school's three variants, each giving up the familiar, the wizard bonus feats or the specialty spell slot: resistance to energy, aura of protection and spontaneous dispelling (abjuration); rapid, enhanced (Augment Summoning instead of Scribe Scroll) and spontaneous summoning (conjuration); enhanced awareness, the bonus feat list and prescience (divination); cohort, social proficiency and extended enchantment (enchantment); energy affinity, energy substitution and overcome resistance (evocation); chains of disbelief, shadow shaper and illusion mastery (illusion); skeletal minion, undead apotheosis and enhanced undead (necromancy); enhance attribute, spell versatility and transmutable memory (transmutation). On the actor the familiar, Scribe Scroll or bonus feats leave (D35E's disabled abilities), a variant that replaces the specialty spell takes away the school slot, the class skills it gives are added (an enchanter's Bluff, Diplomacy, Gather Information, Intimidate and Sense Motive), Augment Summoning is added as a feat, and the rest are notes on the class. A diviner's bonus feat list drops the metamagic feats for Alertness, Blind-Fight, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes and Skill Focus. A variant of another school than the one chosen is ignored.
- **Domain wizard** (Unearthed Arcana): a wizard's Specialist school list on Spells also offers the 13 arcane domains (abjuration, antimagic, battle, cold, conjuration, divination, enchantment, evocation, fire, illusion, necromancy, storm, transmutation). A domain wizard has no prohibited schools; its domain spells go in its spellbook as soon as it can cast them (not counted against the spells it learns; chill metal, control winds and the other non-wizard spells count as wizard spells), and it prepares one domain spell of each level in its special slot. On the actor the domain is a D35E domain feat listing its spells (so the sheet lets them in the slot), and the domain spells are cast at +1 caster level (D35E's caster level offset on each spell). A spontaneous domain wizard casts one more spell a day of each level, for its domain spells. Edit reads it back. World setting **Variant classes** turns it off.
- **Spontaneous casting.** New world setting **Spontaneous casting** (off by default): every caster casts spontaneously. Clerics and druids know spells by Unearthed Arcana's spontaneous divine casters table, with their domain spells (no domain slot) or summon nature's ally of each level, and one more spell a day of each level; paladins and rangers know two more spells of each level than they cast a day; wizards cast any spell in their spellbook (Curios' spellbook too) with their spells a day, nothing prepared. A spontaneous specialist has no school slot: it casts one more spell a day of each spell level, for its school's spells only (its school feat says so; D35E doesn't check the school). Their variants follow (a cloistered cleric too), and a spontaneous wizard counts for the Dragon Disciple's "casts arcane spells without preparation". Off: **Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger and Wizard (Spontaneous)** are variant classes to choose. D35E's class items get its spontaneous spellbook; Edit turns an actor's casters spontaneous when the setting is on. A caster inside a gestalt pairing (its level-0 copy that holds the spellbook) is spontaneous too.
- **Variant classes** (Unearthed Arcana, in the SRD's variant rules), in their own group of the class lists: the totem barbarians (ape, bear, boar, dragon, eagle, horse, lion, serpent, wolf), bardic sage, divine bard, savage bard, cloistered cleric, druidic avenger, thug, the eight monk fighting styles, paladins of freedom, slaughter and tyranny, planar ranger, urban ranger, wilderness rogue and battle sorcerer (data/variants.json).
  - A&M makes each from its standard class: the variant's hit die, base attack, saves, class skills and skill points, alignments, spell tables (a battle sorcerer's one fewer spell a day and known) and spell list (a paladin of tyranny's, the bardic sage's additions), and bonus feats (a bear totem's Toughness, Improved Grapple and Great Fortitude; a monk style's three fixed feats; no 1st-level bonus feat for a thug).
  - On the actor it's a class item named after it ("Barbarian (Bear Totem)") with the standard class's features less those it loses (no fast movement or uncanny dodge for a bear totem), what it gains as notes on the sheet, and D35E changes where they're plain bonuses (+2 on a monk style's skill). Edit reads it back.
  - Random never picks a variant on its own; choose one under Class. New world setting **Variant classes** (on) turns them off.
- **Builds.** A class can have several builds for what A&M chooses: the fighter's sword and board, archer, two-handed, two-weapon and tripper; the rogue's swashbuckler, archer, two-weapon and face; the ranger's archer and two-weapon; the cleric's battle cleric and healer; the wizard's evoker, controller and summoner; the sorcerer's blaster and charmer; the druid's caster and shapeshifter; the paladin's mounted and two-handed; the barbarian's greataxe and spear charger; the monk's kama and unarmed. A build sets the ability order, skills, feats, alignments, spells and gear (an archer's bow gets the better enhancement, a two-handed fighter has a greatsword and no shield, a two-weapon fighter two shortswords).
  - **Random** picks a build for each class, or the one chosen (Build, under the class).
  - **Classes and levels** shows each class's build when it has several; Choose for me, Fill in, Standard gear and Roll again follow it. Review shows it after the class.
  - **Build profiles** (start page, GM): change any class's builds, add your own, hide one of A&M's, or rename and change a class's own build. Kept in the world; Export/Import move them to another world.
- **Class features per level:** Classes and levels shows what each level brings in its Gains column (Sneak Attack +2d6, Evasion, Trap Sense +2, Uncanny Dodge…; click one to read it), and Review lists each class's features with the class level they come at. They come from D35E itself (its Class Abilities by class and level, and the class's own notes), so they match what D35E puts on the sheet; D35E still adds them.
- **Multiclass XP penalty** (SRD): a character with base classes two or more levels apart gets a warning on Classes and levels and Review: −20% XP for each class more than one level below the highest. The race's favored class doesn't count (a human's or half-elf's is its highest-level class), nor do prestige classes and racial hit dice; gestalt characters have none. Classes and levels shows the favored class and the penalty under the table.
  - A character with a penalty gets a **Multiclass XP Penalty (−20%)** trait on the actor, and the XP it gains is reduced by that much (the sheet, a GM's award, a macro), with a note saying so ("+500 XP becomes +400"). The trait follows the classes: when an A&M character gains or changes classes (Edit, or D35E's level-up), its percentage is worked out again, and it leaves when the penalty does. New world setting **Multiclass XP penalty** (on by default) turns it off.
- **Prestige class requirements.** Each prestige class's requirements are checked against the character as it is before the class's first level: the 3.5 SRD's 15 core prestige classes and the psionic SRD's 9 (race, alignment, BAB, skill ranks, feats, spellcasting, spells known, spell schools, manifesting, sneak attack, still mind, martial weapons, languages).
  - A character who doesn't qualify gets a **rule broken** on Classes and levels and Review, listing what's missing ("Level 6: Arcane Trickster needs Escape Artist 7 ranks, sneak attack +2d6."). A requirement A&M can't check (the assassin's kill) is shown as a reminder.
  - The class lists mark a prestige class the character doesn't qualify for at that level with ✘ (point at it to see what's missing); it can still be chosen.
  - **Prestige requirements** (start page, GM): see and change the requirements of every prestige class found, give a homebrew prestige class its own, or set No requirements. Kept in the world (they replace the SRD's); Use the SRD's goes back, and Export/Import move them to another world.
- **Tours brought up to date:** the start page (Recent, the token button, the GM's pages), Random (build, prestige class, owner), Classes and levels (builds, class feature variants, the favored class and XP penalty, the Gains column, variant and prestige classes in the lists), Feats (rogue special abilities, feats given by class features), Spells (arcane domains, spontaneous casting), Equipment (materials, quantities, the actor's own gear when editing) and Review (class features, owner). New GM tours of **Build profiles** and **Prestige requirements**. The sample character a tour shows is now a human ranger 3 / rogue 1, so the combat style, builds and favored class line have something to point at.
- Axecleft's Tools 9.72: searchable lists can have headings that divide them.

## 1.14.0.8
- **Edit: the actor's own gear** (Equipment): each of the actor's items can be kept (the default), **unequipped** (into the pack, so the new levels' gear or treasure can take its place) or **sold**: it leaves the actor and its trade-in value adds to the wealth the new levels bring, to buy the upgrade. Review lists what's unequipped and sold; the copy made before the edit keeps everything.
  - Only a GM sells, unless the new world setting **Players can trade in gear** is on (off by default); players can always keep or unequip.
  - New world setting **Trade-in value**: the % of an item's value it is worth traded in (SRD: half, 50). Gems, art and trade goods count at full value.
- **Working indicator:** while A&M works (Generate, Generate many, Roll again, Choose for me, Standard gear, Fill in what's missing, Create actor, Apply changes) the window shows what it's doing and for how long ("Choosing feats…", "Character 3 of 10: Spells…", "Equipment and treasure… 12 s"), in the look of Shared Data's working label, and waits for it to finish. Random characters are rolled a part at a time so the window keeps drawing.
- **Equipment, more gear:**
  - **Special materials and abilities** on the Add row (with Axecleft's Armory): a material (cold iron, alchemical silver, mithral, adamantine, darkwood…) and special abilities (Flaming, Keen, Fortification…, commas between). The Armory checks them (a slashing-only ability on a mace, a material that can't be used for it) and prices them, and makes the item when the actor is created.
  - **Quantities:** a quantity on the Add row (ammunition 20 when blank), and a quantity box on each item and ammunition row (3 potions of cure light wounds, 50 arrows).
  - **Load:** the gear's weight against the character's Strength and size (SRD Carrying Capacity; Small ×¾), shown under the list. A medium or heavy load is a warning, more than a heavy load a rule broken. Coins aren't counted.
  - **Worn slots:** two items for one slot (two amulets, two cloaks, three rings, two suits of armor) is a warning, by D35E's slots (two rings).
  - Each row shows the item's quantity and weight; the warnings show on Equipment, in its step dot and on Review.
- **Adventuring kits** (Equipment): the class kits of Pathfinder's *Ultimate Equipment* (Open Game Content), made of D35E's own items: backpack, bedroll, belt pouch, flint and steel, iron pot, 5 days of trail rations, soap, 10 torches, waterskin, and each class's own (rope, candles and a wooden holy symbol, holly and mistletoe and animal feed, a spell component pouch, ink and inkpen, a musical instrument and mirror, the rogue's caltrops, chalk, grappling hook, pitons and thieves' tools). A wizard's spellbook is free (a wizard begins play with one). A multiclass or gestalt character gets the kit of its main class plus the other classes' tools. On for PCs, off for NPCs (tick **Adventuring kit**); Standard gear and Random include it. Kit items are carried, not equipped.
- **Small characters** get Small arms, armor and shields: the same price, half the weight, and D35E scales a Small weapon's damage (the Armory makes them when it's active, plain ones too).
- **1st-level PCs** have their class's SRD starting gold (fighter 6d4 × 10 gp, wizard 3d4 × 10 gp, monk 5d4 gp…; a gestalt level, the better of its two classes) instead of the 900 gp of the wealth table: the average, or **Roll** it on Equipment. What the gear doesn't use is rolled as treasure as before.
- **Tours** of the steps show a sample character (a 4th-level human fighter; a cleric 2 / wizard 2 for Spells) when the character being built has no levels yet, so every step points at its part instead of the middle of the window. The character being built comes back when the tour ends, and the sample is never saved. The Skills, Classes and Spells tours point at the table's first row (a whole table could push the step's box off screen), and the skill Level step opens below its buttons.
- **Players build too.** Players can open A&M (the Axecleft's Tools menu, and the Build a character button in the Actors directory), build and randomize characters, and edit the actors they own (their right-click menu and sheet).
  - A player without Foundry's **Create New Actors** permission (players don't have it by default) gets the new actor made by a GM's Foundry: a GM needs to be logged in. The actor belongs to that player, and the player's window fills it in as usual. Edit's copy before an edit is made the same way, owned by the player. The start page tells players how it works.
  - New world setting **Players build through the GM** (on by default): untick it and only users with the Create New Actors permission can make actors; everyone can still edit what they own.
  - Players only see the compendiums they have permission to see.
- **Edit an actor.** From the start page (pick an actor, a selected token, or drop one on the window), an actor's right-click menu in the Actors directory, or its sheet's ⋮ menu: **Edit with Adventurers and Minions**. The actor is read into the steps; change anything, then **Apply changes** on Review.
  - Actors A&M made reopen exactly as they were built (their plan is kept on the actor). Other actors are read from what they have: race and class items, D35E's level records (or, without level-up progression, the class items' levels with the hit points shared out and the skill ranks spread over the levels in the order of classes that fits them best), feats placed in their slots, spells by their spellbook's class. What A&M guessed is said at the top of Classes and levels and Review; anything it doesn't know (a homebrew feat, a class not in your compendiums, spells inside Curios' spellbook items) stays on the actor untouched.
  - **Fill in what's missing** (Review): skills of the levels with points left, empty feat slots and feats whose prerequisites are no longer met (a feat whose slot went with a changed class moves to a free slot or goes), ability increases and spells of new levels, by the class profiles. What you chose stays.
  - **Changes** (Review) lists everything Apply will do. Apply writes only what changed: class items and D35E's level records, ability scores, skill ranks, the feats and spells added or removed, prepared counts. Gear, effects, class features and everything else stay. XP is moved into the new level (characters), and full hit points stay full.
  - A **copy** of the actor is made first, in the folder **A&M backups** (setting **Copy before an edit**, on by default; it can be turned off on Review).
  - Equipment: gear added on the step is added to the actor; treasure can be rolled for the wealth the new levels bring (arms only where nothing is equipped). Spells: **Add spells for new levels** keeps the actor's spells.
- **Domains and specialist schools** (Spells step), from D35E's Spell Schools and Domains compendium.
  - Clerics choose two domains (alignment domains need a cleric of that alignment, SRD). Their domain spells are added to the list and tagged **domain**, and each spell level from 1st gets **+1 domain slot**: pick the spell for it with **slot**.
  - Wizards can specialize in a school and tick the schools they give up (two, or one for a diviner). Spells of prohibited schools are never learned (any already chosen are taken off), and each spell level from 1st gets **+1 specialty slot** for a spell of the school.
  - Spells that aren't on their spellbook class's own list (domain spells, spells from other compendiums) go on without D35E's "Spell added despite not being in a spell list for class" warning for each one.
  - The actor gets the domain or school feats (D35E's sheet then marks domain, school and prohibited spells) and the slot spells in D35E's special slots. Edit reads them back and changes them.
  - Random characters and **Choose for me** pick domains and schools by the class profiles (clerics' favourite domains for their alignment; wizards specialize half the time). Review shows them and warns about a missing domain, a prohibited school too many or too few, or an empty slot.
- **Gestalt characters** (Unearthed Arcana, in the SRD's variant rules): tick **Gestalt** on Classes and levels (or on the random page) and each level takes two classes.
  - On the actor each pairing is one class A&M builds from the two, named "Fighter // Wizard": the better hit die, base attack bonus, saves and skill points, both class skill lists and both classes' features (each at its own class level, from D35E's Class Abilities). No custom classes are needed in your compendiums. Its description and flags note the two classes it was built from.
  - Spellcasting stays with the casting class: a copy of it at level 0 carries its spell table, and the gestalt class gives a **Bonus Arcane/Divine Spellcaster Level** at every level, which A&M spends on that class's spellbook. A Cleric // Wizard 5 casts as a 5th-level cleric and a 5th-level wizard, and the caster's levels carry on when the other side changes.
  - Bonus feats, prerequisites (class levels) and alignment follow each class by its own level. Random characters, Choose for me, Edit and Review handle gestalt levels.
  - **Standard gear** for a gestalt class: arms and armour from the class with the better base attack bonus, plus the other class's worn magic items for the slots left empty (a Fighter // Wizard's headband; no bracers of armor over armour).
- **Psionic powers** (Spells step): psions, wilders and psychic warriors get their powers known by the class tables, of levels they can learn, from D35E's Powers compendium. A psion's list is the psion/wilder list plus its discipline's (Psion (Egoist): egoist powers), with a discipline power among each level's new powers; class profiles give favourite powers (Mind Thrust, Force Screen, Inertial Armor…). The step shows powers known against the table, the highest power level and about how many power points a day; Review warns about too many powers or one above the level the class can learn. Powers go on as D35E powers (spontaneous, their power point cost), and D35E works out the power points.
  - Each psionic spellbook's daily power points formula is set so the sheet shows its points and a rest refills them: with one psionic class, the actor's whole pool (@attributes.powerPointsTotal, which D35E adds up from the classes and feats); with several, each spellbook after the first gets its own class's points and the first the rest of the pool, so every class can manifest and the total stays the pool. A formula the GM wrote is left alone. A new character starts with its points full.
  - A gestalt class no longer counts power points of its own (it kept the first class's power point table, so a Psion // anything counted the psion's points twice).
  - A second psionic class (Psion // Wilder, two disciplines) learns other powers than the first: the character can manifest the first's powers anyway, so this widens its repertoire.
  - Gestalt: a psionic class goes on at level 0 like the other casting classes (its powers in its own spellbook), with a locked class feature, **Gestalt Psionics: Psion (Nomad)**, that adds the power points of its gestalt levels to the pool (the class table plus the key ability's bonus, kept as a live formula) and as many psionic bonus manifester levels, spent on its spellbook. Psion // Wilder and a psionic class across two pairings get all their points; Edit rebuilds the feature when the levels change. (D35E's spellbook header still counts powers known and the highest power level from the level-0 copy; A&M's Spells step and Review check them against the tables.)
- Gear A&M places (armour, shield, weapons, worn items) is equipped on the actor: D35E treated the new items as transferred and left them unequipped.
- A random gestalt character's skills alternate between its two classes' favourites (a Fighter // Rogue gets Climb and Search, not only fighter skills).
- Random point buy follows the class profile again: the first ability to 16, then the next ones, while points last (it was raising every score a point at a time, leaving them all near 12).
- Each spellbook gets the right class when there are several new class items (they're matched by A&M's notes and compendium source, not by the order Foundry returns them in): a Wizard // Cleric's wizard spells no longer land in the cleric's book.
- **Tours** of every page (Configure Settings → Tour Management, or the window's ⋮ menu → **Tour of this tab**): the start page, Random, Edit an actor, Basics, Abilities, Classes and levels (gestalt), Skills, Feats, Spells (domains, schools, powers), Equipment and Review. Players can take them too.
- Ships Axecleft's Tools 9.71 (tours, Foundry 11 too).
## 1.14.0.7
- Spellbook items: when Curios' spellbook rule is on, the classes it lists (Wizard by default) get their spells written in spellbook items (100 pages each) instead of loose on the actor. The books are added first, Curios puts their spells in the class's spellbook, then the day's spells are prepared from them. The Spells step says so.

## 1.14.0.6
- Clerics, druids, paladins, rangers and adepts get their whole spell list: every SRD spell of the levels they can cast (some prepared), plus random non-SRD spells from your other compendiums at each of those levels. New setting **Non-SRD spells per caster level** (default 1; 0 for SRD only).
- Wizards' spellbooks get spells found adventuring: at each spell level 1, plus 1 for every 2 levels since they gained it, mostly SRD. Their free spells (and sorcerers' and bards' spells known) are mostly SRD. Non-SRD spells are marked on the Spells step.
- Spells go on the actor a compendium at a time (a cleric's whole list is hundreds of spells).
- Treasure arms: armor, shields and weapons are kept only when the character can use them (class, race and feat proficiencies; druids no metal; no shield with a two-handed weapon) and only the best of each: one armor, one shield, one melee and one ranged weapon. Better is the higher effective bonus (enhancement plus special abilities), then special abilities, then price; the Weapon Focus weapon counts +1. What's replaced goes back into the wealth. Magic ammunition only for a bow, crossbow or sling the character has.
- Psionic treasure only for characters with a psionic class.
